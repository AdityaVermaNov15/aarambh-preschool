Who we are

This app is run by Aarambh Pre School, under the auspices of R.L.J.D. Group, [school address]. Contact for privacy questions: [privacy contact name], [email], [phone].

What we collect

  • Parents, guardians and staff: name, mobile number, role, and a password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Children: name, class, date of birth, gender, blood group, admission number, profile photo, attendance, fee records, awards, and notes such as allergies or pickup person, entered by the school or a parent.
  • Parents' details: father's and mother's names, phone numbers, occupations, email, address and an emergency contact.
  • School activity photos and videos posted by teachers, and helpdesk messages.
  • Device data for alerts: if you turn on phone alerts, a notification address for your device.

We do not collect location, contacts, advertising IDs or browsing history, and the app has no ads or trackers.

Why we use it

Only to run the school: taking attendance, sharing notices, meal and class plans, date sheets and holidays, answering helpdesk requests, recording fees, and sharing class moments with families. We do not sell or rent any data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Children's information and parental consent

The app is used by parents, guardians and school staff, not by children directly. A parent or guardian gives consent when signing up. Photos and videos are visible only to school staff and, when a teacher shares them, to parents of children in the same class. Parents can see only their own child's records.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in a PostgreSQL database and private file storage run on Supabase, in the [region, e.g. Mumbai, India] data centre. Access is protected by sign-in and database security rules. Photos are shown through links that expire after one day. If you use the app from another country, your information is still stored in that region.

Who can see it

  • School admin and teachers: records needed for their work.
  • Parents: their own child's records, notices meant for parents, and posts shared with their child's class or the whole school.
  • Service providers: Supabase (hosting) and, for phone alerts, your phone maker's notification service (Apple, Google or your browser vendor), which receives only the alert text.

How long we keep it

While your child is enrolled and for [period, e.g. 3 years] afterwards for school records, unless the law requires longer. Stories disappear after 24 hours.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account at any time in the app: Me → Delete my account. This removes your login, name, phone number and alert devices.
  • Ask the school to see, correct or delete your or your child's information, or to remove a photo, by contacting [email]. We respond within [30] days.
  • Depending on where you live, laws such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, the EU/UK GDPR, or the US COPPA may give you further rights. You may also complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post a notice in the app and update the date above.
